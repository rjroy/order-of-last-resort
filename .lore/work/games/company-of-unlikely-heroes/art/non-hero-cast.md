# Barnaby’s Order of Last Resort: non-hero battle cast

**Inventory baseline:** `629a36a`. Across 20 playable missions the stock bundle places 26 distinct non-party character IDs: **25 living characters and one moving objective, the Supply Wagon**. Two additional non-hero IDs, Skeleton and Zombie, enter through summons and already have approved models and working battle animations. Seven recruitable heroes are out of scope. This is an art inventory, not a change to mission design or approval of a concept. Beads tracks production status; the character ID, not the displayed label or placement name, is the join key. Epic: mind-garage-xmy.

## Production standard

Prefer a distinct silhouette, posture, kit and characteristic motion per identity. Shared rigs, cameras and bake tooling are welcome; palette swaps alone are not a substitute for an identifiable character. Repeated placements of the same character can share a model. Use the existing Crooked Storybook Fantasy direction. Follow concept → approval → editable rig and poses → approval → baked sprites and actual Godot mission integration. Preserve older props or authored custom art where needed. Show the final result on the board at tactical size, rather than signing off from Blender alone.

## Already delivered and scope boundaries

For the cast that follows the rescue pair, the [accepted model roster](concepts/non-hero-cast/accepted-roster.json) identifies the exact approved Blender sources and the five superseded versions. A roster entry records approval, not delivery. For each delivered actor below, launch its listed stock mission normally or run the [stock graphical regression](../../../../../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tests/stock_cast_mission_test.md) with its mission and character IDs and a fresh local capture directory.

- **Goblin (`goblin`, xmy.3):** `goblin_mob` fields five copies of the same [approved rig](models/goblin/README.md). Legal committed rounds exercise scamper and jab through the actual battle UI. Run the graphical regression with `--mission=goblin_mob --actor=goblin`.
- **Hookhand (`hookhand`, xmy.4):** The [approved hook and towline rig](models/hookhand/README.md) reels at native F18 and recovers from an actual knockdown. Use `--mission=harpoon_squad --actor=hookhand` and `python games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tools/cast/verify_cast.py hookhand`.
- **Bruiser (`bruiser`, xmy.5):** The [two-handed maul](models/bruiser/README.md) lands on frame 18 of Heavy Strike. Use `--mission=harpoon_squad --actor=bruiser` for the normal encounter capture, with the rest of the squad present.
- **Rival Knight (`rival_knight`, xmy.16):** The [approved shield-bearing rig](models/rival_knight/README.md) uses its native shield push. Verify the stock board with `--mission=mirror_company --actor=rival_knight`; verify its packaged atlas with `python games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tools/cast/verify_cast.py rival_knight`.
- **Rival Wizard (`rival_wizard`, xmy.17):** The [approved stovepipe rig](models/rival_wizard/README.md) aims its blast at the resolved target with feedback on native F10. Use `--mission=mirror_company --actor=rival_wizard` and `python games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tools/cast/verify_cast.py rival_wizard`.
- **Rival Healer (`rival_healer`, xmy.18):** The [approved medic rig](models/rival_healer/README.md) uses distinct ally heal and padded bonk takes. Use `--mission=mirror_company --actor=rival_healer`, adding `--variant=bonk` for the later attack, and `python games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tools/cast/verify_cast.py rival_healer`.
- **Enemy Healer (`enemy_healer`, xmy.12):** The [approved field-medic rig](models/enemy_healer/README.md) uses separate bonk and field-dressing takes. Use `--mission=unfortunate_healer --actor=enemy_healer`, adding `--variant=field_dressing` for a positive ally heal, and `python games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tools/cast/verify_cast.py enemy_healer`.

- **Harpooner (`harpooner`, xmy.1):** [Approved concept](concepts/harpooner/README.md) and [editable master and bake recipe](models/harpooner/README.md). The stock rescue regression commits legal rounds for close thrust, ranged cast and projectile flight, pursuit, hit and defeat. The binding check for all four stock missions is `godot --headless --path games/company-of-unlikely-heroes --script res://tests/harpooner_binding_test.gd`.
- **Ferryman (`ferryman`, xmy.2):** [Approved concept](concepts/ferryman/README.md), [editable master and bake recipe](models/ferryman/README.md), and [standalone and adventure capture instructions](../../../../../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tests/ferryman_rescue_scene_test.md). ## How to recheck coverage

From `games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/content/catalog.json`, merge the listed parts; follow `missions[*].encounter` to every `encounters[*].placements[*].character`, including placements held in reserve. Resolve each character against its own record and class defaults. Check summon ability definitions separately and compare `cast_key` against `games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/assets/cast/manifest.json`. Character definitions come from `common.json`, `village_troubles.json`, `frontier_combats.json` and `frontier_objectives.json`; `first_outing.json` provides opening placements. Recheck this list when a new mission introduces a new identity.
