# A Second Set of Heroes

## Dainty Bruiser

Bruiser by build. Dainty by nature. Best visual reference is "Odd Job" from one of the James Bond movies.

- abilities
  - "Let Me Clean That Up" - The fastidious dainty bruiser will clean up the wound of a fellow hero (recover 1 HP of a hero, range 2)
  - "This Might Sting" - Still a bruiser, the dainty bruiser warns the opponet before knocking them senseless (deal 1 HP and prevent next action, range adjacent)
- default move action - pursue
- standing order (this might sting, targeting opponent in range)

## Composed Berserker

This berserker has learned the art of relaxation and no longer enters blind rages.

- abilities
  - "Non-Reactive" - Stand there and take the hits. (All damage received will be applied to the next damaging attack by the berserker. This will continue to accumulate until damage is dealt.)
  - "Wide Swing" - Cleaves their axe in front of them. (deal 1 HP base plus all accumulated non-reactive damage. The target is one adjacent opponent and any opponent adjacent to that opponent.)
- default move action - pursue
- standing order (non-reactive)

## Bashful Banneret

Carries the guilds banner, but really doesn't like to be the spot light of attention. The banneret still has great pride in the guild.

- abilities
  - "This old thing?" - Feeling noticed, raises banner high to draw the attention away from themselves. (inspire nearest ally. this generally means +1 to next ability use)
  - "Back-off!" - Doesn't like to be crowded. (standard push action)
- default move action - retreat
- standing order (this old thing?)

## Skeptical Oracle

Has never understood the visions they have. Sure everything is predicted for them, but what's about to happen is just obvious anyone could've predicted it.

- abilities
  - "Predictable" - That move was so obvious, there's no way it was going to work. (next attack of the targeted enemy has no effect)
  - "You Should've Looked" - Trying to explain that preperation matters. "You should've looked before you lept" is the full explaination they give before causing their opponent to land behind them. (move targeted opponent to the opposite side of the oracle, also cause the opponent to be prone, and take 1 HP)
- default move action - retreat
- standing order (predictable, closest opponent)

## Stuff Animal Wrangler

This animal wrangler has two problems, they never quite grew up and they are afraid of real animals. Still they have managed to turn this into something maybe useful.

- abilities
  - "Summon (Teddy) Bear" - Positions a giant life sized teddy bear, which has a rather annoying music box within it. (Summon the teddy bear on a targeted square, 1 bear at a time, the bear has 2HP, and cannot move, it will cause a taunt to the closes opponent each round)
  - "Throw Meanie Babies" - Not entirely sure how, but the wrangler will throw 50 small plushies at the targeted opponent. It's such a mess that the opponent trips right on their face. (opponet becomes prone and takes 1 HP, range 3)
- default move - hold
- standing order ("Throw Meanie Babies" at closest opponent)

## Gullible Warlock

Completely unaware of the evil intent of their patron. This warlock freely uses the corrupting abilities. But the warlock is so gullible they don't really seemed to be influenced, much to the dismay of their demonic patron.

- abilities
  - "Have You Met?" - The warlock can open a quick portal to let their patron say hello. Seeing a demon is not something one normally wants and causes the opponent to flee. (acts as a ranged push at a targeted opponent, range 3)
  - "Ouch Blast" - The warlock has been gifted a rather powerful blasting ability, but they don't really understand why it hurts so much [maybe because they haven't given in to the evil]. (range 3, deal 3 HP to targeted opponent, but takes 1 HP in backlash)
- default move - hold
- standing order ("Have You Met?" nearest opponent)

## Conspicuous Scout

This scout is completely color blind. So instead of wearing greens and browns they wear reds and blues. They are also completely oblivious to their own noise.

- abilities
  - "I'm Not Here" - Rather than avoid notice, they pronounce their presence. (taunt all opponents within 2 squares, the move towards or attack the scout)
  - "Bow" - standard archer thing. (range 3 squares, 2 HP)
- default move - follow ally
- standing order - "I'm Not Here"

## Queasy Surgeon

they are the best around. Fast, efficient, and vomits after every visit.

- abilities
  - "Field Medic" - In a flash, they'll heal the entire troupe, but they'll need a moment after words to themselves. (range 3 squares, all allies +1 HP, but then the surgeon is prone, and must spend the next turn standing)
  - "Break Bone" - They also know how to break bone. (adjacent oppoonent, damage 2 HP)
