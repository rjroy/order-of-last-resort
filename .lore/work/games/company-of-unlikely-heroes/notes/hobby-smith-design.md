# Hobby Smith: Try This

**Design proposal for `olr-djp.5`.** Bring the Smith when you want the Knight to pull an enemy *toward* the company, not because the Knight needs more damage. The price of that new option is a party slot and a Smith activation. The payoff is that you can still spend the company's one command on somebody else.

Ronald's [original Blacksmith pitch](hero-class-pitches.md) supplied pursuit, the Push, Guard and Blast examples, and Back Off (1 damage and a shove). This revision uses **Hobby Smith**, the established display name, and **Hammer Time** instead of Back Off. Ronald's subsequent direction supplies the nearest-hero starting order, freely chosen manual recipient, Dodger and Bard examples, and the idea that upgrades can make the converted ability more effective. The Recall Skeleton and Play Dead interactions are assistant proposals. Names, numbers, secondary interactions, purchases and rewards proposed below are design candidates. The Smith is not a stock recruit; this document neither changes the game nor approves production.

## Recruitment card

| Slot | First kit |
|---|---|
| Offense: **Hammer Time** | Pick an adjacent living opponent or damageable obstacle. Deal **2 damage**, no shove, collision bonus or taunt. A redirectable attack, not direct HP loss. |
| Utility: **Try This** | Fit one other living deployed hero, anywhere on the board. Their next eligible performed ability uses its named alternate once. No line of sight, adjacency or empty equipment slot required. |
| Default equipped standing order: **Try This** | Fit the nearest other living deployed hero. If the fitting cannot be applied, hold. The Smith does not pursue, move or swing on the fitting activation. |
| Movement habit / selectable order: **Pursue** | Target the nearest living opponent. Use the equipped offense on them when legal; otherwise step one legal tile toward its reach. The step ends the activation even if the opponent is now in reach. Hold if neither is possible. Don't smash a crate or pick a patient to keep busy. |
| Also available | Standing **Hold** does nothing. Direct Move is the ordinary one-tile move; it replaces, not supplements, whichever order was equipped. |

Start paper testing at **8 HP**, the stock Knight's starting maximum. The test is whether a Smith can afford to spend a turn tinkering within reach of trouble. Armor, hammer knockback and a fourth party slot would muddy that question.

## One fitting, one action

- One active **Fitted** mark per Smith; no stacks. Manually fitting a different hero moves the mark; show who loses it. Fitting the same hero again is unavailable. Standing Try This holds if its nearest hero already wears the fitting; it does not quietly fit the runner-up. Distance is orthogonal; ties between heroes go to earlier deployment slot. Summons, civilians, enemies, corpses, objects and the Smith are never recipients.
- A fitting lasts across rounds until used, replaced, the wearer falls, or the mission ends. The Smith falling does not undo a fitting already delivered. Carry neither fittings nor Inspiration into another mission. A skipped activation, direct Move, Hold, movement-only order, shield pickup, or failed attempt never spends it.
- On the wearer's activation, resolve **the ability they actually elect to use**, whether directly commanded or supplied by their order. Offer the alternate's *own* legal targets first, not the original ability's targets. Thus ranged Pull can be performed even when no normal Push is in reach, and Recall can be performed while the skeleton slot is full. Do not change the equipped ability, the standing order or the initiative list.
- If there is **no legal alternate**, the ordinary ability may resolve against an ordinary legal target and **the mark stays**. If neither is legal, wait or take the order's normal movement branch. Do not silently substitute the base attack just because it does more damage, and do not fall back against a different unit after a directly selected target becomes invalid. Display the ordinary branch by name, especially when a supposed healing song will instead hurt someone.
- A turn that successfully performs the alternate spends the mark before applying its effects. Resistance, a shield absorbing the hit, or a patient healing for less than the nominal amount does not refund it. Primary ability only: never convert a reflection, retaliation, greeting by another actor, echo, reaction, splash, follow-up step, collision, or death effect. Generated effects cannot generate another fitting, alternate activation or echo.

**What the player can actually order.** With the Smith deployed first on standing Try This, the player can command the Knight's converted Pull this round. With the Smith manually fitting the Knight, the Knight must use their standing order this round. With the Knight deployed first, a newly applied fitting cannot rewrite the Knight's past turn: it waits for a later activation. Replacing the Smith's standing Try This with Hammer Time can therefore change the Knight's legal action. Never prompt for a second command during commit.

**Forecast contract.** Derive the command picker's legal actions from the board immediately before the selected hero's slot, simulating earlier standing orders. For automatic or delegated abilities the forecast chooses the target, never the player. For an aimed direct ability, show only legal alternate targets, or explicitly label the ordinary fallback if there are none. Clearing/changing the command restarts the simulated round from the same state. Commit replays that plan; no preview writes marks, moves pieces, advances random streams, spends the bottle or rolls another song listener. Show cause, wearer, base → alternate name, targets, amounts, landings and any mark left over. Example: `Smith fits Knight → Advance uses Pull: Bruiser D2 to C2 → Bruiser cannot reach Healer`. For a wait: `Guard unavailable: shield away. Fitted stays.`

## The conversion register

The rows below are the contract, not examples of an automatic “opposite” algorithm. Use the acting hero's effective source values after their own equipped kit and earned upgrades. No borrowed ability grants its donor's rewards, passive or orders.

**Reading the rows.** Hero means a living deployed player hero. Friendly character includes allied summons and friendly civilians only where specified. Damage requires a damageable target; healing is capped at missing HP, with no revival. “Nearest” uses legal candidates and orthogonal distance, then encounter order unless specified. Random means a seeded uniform draw from eligible candidates, shown exactly in forecast. For aimed alternates used by standing orders, use the stated preference; absent another rule, choose the nearest legal candidate, then encounter order. A move needs an enterable destination. A one-tile shove or pull takes the dominant axis, horizontal on a tie. No diagonal movement, chains or collision damage unless expressly stated.

**Bottle identity.** A newly administered converted dose with Keep the Culture stores its resolved form, underlying recipe ID, reaction and recipient. Ordinary Repeat reproduces that form; Counterbatch flips it once. Example: converted Acid heals a hero but bottles a harmful reaction. Counterbatch damages an opponent at Acid range and delivers that same harmful reaction; it does not make another bottle. Use the usual dose roll tied to performer and draw number, not a new roll for each target. Ordinary/potion odds are 70% beneficial, 20% harmful, 10% cosmetic; Acid swaps beneficial and harmful weights. Conversion selects its declared pool, never searches for a favorable result.

## Inspiration and upgrades

**Inspired** is not a second fitting. It does not change which ability a hero performs. Apply existing upgrades, choose ordinary or fitted form, then improve the resolved primary ability once:

- For a damage, heal or hit-reduction amount, add **1** to the primary amount; a primary area uses that amount for each primary recipient. Do not add it to collision, self-injury, ricochets, retaliation, reactions or echoes. For Raise Dead or a newly summoned skeleton, add 1 starting/current maximum HP to that summon only.
- For any other performed ability, grant its actor **Steady Hands**: reduce their next incoming hit by 1 through the start of their next activation. Positioning, target count, duration and turn order stay as printed. Thus ordinary Hammer Time hits for 3; inspired Try This fits **one** hero normally and protects the Smith, never inspiring its wearer. An inspired converted Melody spends the old Inspiration before choosing a listener and grants no extra Inspiration from the bonus. Ordinary Melody can make an enemy laugh and protect the singer; it never starts a second song.
- Move, Hold, skips and invalid actions retain Inspiration. An ordinary ability that actually performs on the fallback branch can spend Inspiration while retaining Fitted. A legal dry song counts as performed. No duplicate Inspiration, no repeated protection from the same token, no boost to later automatic events; the forecast names the bonus. Multiple reductions on the same hit cap at that hit's damage. If the listener plans Move, show the pending token, not a fictitious improved Move.

**Upgrade contract for the wearer.** Include the values and conditions that still make sense; don't invisibly run the original hostile effect under the friendly one. In addition to the numeric inheritance in the register:

| Family | Keep / intentionally leave inactive on a converted use |
|---|---|
| Knight | Wide Guard enlarges the shove radius. Shield damage scales Lend a Shield, not the harmless area shove. Dig In still needs actual guarding. Retrieving is not a throw and does not spend the mark. |
| Healer | Long Staff and Bonk damage improve the protective tap. Strong Swing covers each *other* legal hero in reach once. Heal amount improves Doctor's Orders. Me or You's self/split healing requires an ordinary Heal. |
| Caster | More Powder increases Mending Blast healing; Conductive Personality sends half (round up) to the nearest other wounded hero once. No Demolition Practice or Duck! trigger on a heal; suppress Parting Gift's hostile shove explicitly. Recoil-specific Padded Landing and Braced Casting do nothing on Send-Off. |
| Dodger | Cheap Shot, Long Finger and Pass the Buck need a blame mark/redirect, not a taunt. Clear Jump swaps nobody: no Body Double, Pocket Sand or Borrowed Courage. Exit Strategy still improves ordinary Retreat. |
| Bard | Sharper Notes raises healing; Front Row Seats limits the relevant Sonnet/Chorus primary draw to the two lowest-current-HP legal patients, including cutoff ties. No Heckler on a healed hero. No Laughing Fit on Inspiration. Busker's Hat still applies to a dry ordinary Melody. For Song Weaving, count the performed source song for consecutive-round history; the one echo is always the **ordinary** previous song, with its normal listener and immunity, never fitted or inspired. | Crypter | Recall retains the actual skeleton, including Spare Ribs; Play Dead preserves the original enemy maximum, not a Good Soil boosted zombie maximum. Max-HP upgrades apply normally. The forecast should say why a familiar perk is idle (e.g., `Pocket Sand: no swap`), rather than claiming all upgrades work identically. The defining trade is an alternate use, not every base perk plus a second complete action.

## A Knight turn worth fitting

Hand-drawn corridor, not a claimed stock encounter. Initiative **Smith, Knight, Healer, Bruiser**. Smith A2, Knight B2, Bruiser D2, wounded Healer E2, C2 free. The Bruiser will hit an adjacent hero, preferring the Healer if in reach. Assume the Bruiser survives all attacks and has one activation, not move-and-strike.

1. **Without a fitting:** Advance cannot Push from B2. The Knight steps to C2; the Bruiser stays beside the wounded Healer. Commanding the Healer to flee would save her but costs her Support this round.
2. **Smith on standing Try This:** the Knight is the nearest hero. Advance now finds a legal Pull from B2: D2 → C2. The Bruiser ends next to the Knight, two tiles from the Healer. The Healer keeps Support, and the direct command is still free. The Knight accepts the danger; this is not just a stronger hit.
3. **Change one tile:** put a crate at C2. No open first pull step on the Bruiser. Advance does not pull through the crate. The forecast now shows its ordinary pursuit/wait branch and the unspent fitting. A direct Knight Pull on some other legal piece is a different plan, not an automatic free rescue.

For Guard, place the Knight C3 on a ritual circle with a friend B3 holding a second circle, an enemy D3 and open A3/E3. Ordinary Guard leaves both circles occupied; fitted Guard shoves friend and foe to A3/E3. It can clear an attacker **and cost the ritual streak**. If the Smith is about to deliver the fitting, directly command the Smith to move instead: the Knight performs ordinary Guard and both circle holders stay put. If the fitting already exists, a direct Knight Move to an open C4 would bank it and spare the friend, but **vacate C3**. This is why the friendly arrows and objective aftermath belong in the forecast.

## Progression proposal

**Guild:** propose Recruit the Hobby Smith at 5 points, with Hammer Time, Try This, Pursue, standing Try This and Hold immediately usable. Offer **both** offense petitions separately: **Learn Bonk and Taunt** (4; requires Smith) and **Learn Acid** (5; requires Smith and recruited Mixologist). Each replaces Hammer Time only when equipped; Try This stays. Bonk trades a simple hit for enemy attention; Acid trades adjacent 2 for range-3 damage 1 and a forecast reaction. Pursue uses the equipped reach. Neither petition imports the donor's orders, rewards or other slot. Costs are comparison knobs, not approved prices.

**Outing:** revise the [historical Back Off pool](between-mission-rewards.md) rather than promising a push on a hammer that no longer pushes. Proposed offers start next mission, respect per-outing caps, and vanish on guild return. Gate hammer-only offers on equipped Hammer Time.

| Rarity / cap | Offer | What actually changes |
|---|---|---|
| Common ×3 | Leather Apron | +1 maximum and current HP without removing existing wounds. |
| Common ×2 | Heavier Hammer | +1 Hammer Time damage per copy; not Bonk or Acid damage. |
| Uncommon ×1 | Follow Through | If Hammer Time defeats its adjacent target, step onto that tile if still enterable after defeat effects. Forecast the landing; no extra attack. |
| Uncommon ×2 | Reinforced Rivets | While a new fitting remains, reduce its wearer's next hit by 1 per copy; consumed on that hit or lost when the mark ends. Repeated standing waits do not renew it. |
| Rare ×1 | Fine Adjustment | Make the **performed alternate** more effective using the Inspiration rule above: Mending Blast heals 5 instead of 4 before other upgrades; Clear Jump gives its jumper Steady Hands. Applies on actual conversion only, never on an ordinary fallback. If the wearer was already Inspired, spend that token normally and apply the improvement only once. Grants no Inspiration token, extra fitting or extra activation; a converted song cannot pass the improvement along. Unlike Rivets, this changes the wearer's action, not their protection while waiting. Replaces **Long Tongs**: unlimited manual recipient range makes +1 range a dead offer. |
| Rare ×1 | Quick Release | Optional direct command for the wearer: explicitly remove their fitting, then make an ordinary legal one-tile Move. Cannot select without a legal move. Regular Move still retains the mark. |
| Legendary ×1 | Double Shift | Permit two different fitted teammates. Each spends independently; never stack on one hero. Standing still considers only the nearest hero, not the nearest unmarked one. In a three-hero party, fitting the second often takes the Smith's direct command. |

## Coverage audit and decision gate

For evidence from the implemented game, see the [Hobby Smith verification record](hobby-smith-verification.md). The inventory and gate below preserve the original design proposal.

Current inventory, relative to `games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/`:

- `content/common.json` supplies 14 unique starting actions across the seven kits named in `content/guild_progression.json`. Common `move` is explicitly exempt, as are `wave`, `dont_go`, `say_hello`, `offer_flower` (summons) and `enemy_attack`, `enemy_harpoon` (opponents). Encounter-only enemy actions, rival copies, objective events, passive techniques and shield pickup are not commandable hero abilities.
- `content/guild_progression.json` identifies abilities granted by petitions; the six new definitions live in `content/guild_training.json`. Reusing seven existing IDs creates more eligible wielders, not more conversions. `content/rewards.json` grants five additional distinct actions, including Repeat Batch, not just the more visible Plain Tonic. **Total: 25 rows for 25 distinct stock non-Move hero abilities.** Proposed Hammer Time and Try This need no fitted forms because the Smith cannot fit themself and stock recruitment forbids duplicate heroes. Future heroes/custom content need explicit entries or a disclosed ordinary-only fallback, not inferred inverses.
- `rules/actions.gd` (ownership, choices, limits and target validation), `rules/behaviors.gd` (orders select actual abilities or move/wait), `rules/upgrades.gd` (actor-specific effective values), `rules/alchemy.gd` (recipe, recipient, exact reaction and emptying), `rules/performances.gd`, `rules/shield.gd` and `rules/summons.gd` establish existing interactions. The [game guide](../../../../../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/README.md) describes the one-command round, initiative, carryover and player-facing forecast. The alternates above are **not** in those files.

**Paper checks before promising roster support.** Compare fitted and unfitted plans on the same board and initiative. Check earlier/later Smith, direct versus standing, blocked landing, no patient, shield away, tied recipients and changing a preview. Test the marked Make a Friend with and without its skeleton; Play Dead on a ritual tile with a zombie already due to act; Counterbatch with two friendly candidates, a bad stored reaction and an ordinary fallback; a woven enemy echo following a friendly song; and a Smith inspired by the Bard while actively fitting someone. Verify objectives and standard entry/defeat effects on every landing: a vacated plate can shut a gate, a bridge can still collapse, displacing a civilian can fail a rescue. No new trap tiles are part of this kit. For a future entry-triggered trap, use the landing tile's ordinary trigger once per actual entrant, not one trigger per crossed tile or per preview refresh.

**Tune the costly decisions first.** Test Pull reach 2 versus 3, Guard friends included versus hostile-only, whether repeated fitting crowds out Hammer Time, and how often a no-patient fallback threatens a protected NPC. Check whether unlimited direct fitting and the nearest standing restriction each create a plan worth making. Keep the three-hero party and one-command limit fixed. Ask Ronald to predict the landing/victim *before* reading the full forecast, then whether he would choose the Smith again for that board. Repeated surprise at fallback, routine hero-on-Hold just to save a fitting, or one universally preferred standing order are reasons to revise. The catalog audit establishes coverage, **not** readability, balance or enjoyment. Next decision: approve, revise or reject the Knight paper pairing and the named alternates before authorizing any prototype, roster change or art.
