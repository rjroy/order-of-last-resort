# Hobby Smith sound-effect release terms

## Key Findings

- Local Mirli reported an installed audio.cpp Stable Audio 3.0 Small SFX model under the Stability AI Community License, with conditional commercial use. Local auditioning does not establish production or release clearance.
- The [checkpoint terms](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-audio-3-small-sfx/blob/main/LICENSE.md) distinguish standalone output from Model Derivative Works. Between the user and Stability AI, the user owns output to the extent permitted by law. The terms still require legal and acceptable-use compliance for output and disclaim title and non-infringement warranties.
- Commercial use of the Stability AI Materials requires registration under §III. If aggregate annual revenue of the licensee and affiliates exceeds US $1 million, the agreement says to request an Enterprise license. The project owner's applicable eligibility and registration have not been verified.
- The agreement's license-copy, notice and “Powered by Stability AI” provisions address distribution of model materials or derivatives, including products or services using them. Do not assume they automatically apply to a standalone WAV, or that an MIT repository license independently clears that WAV. Have the release owner confirm the actual distribution scenario and applicable terms before publishing candidate audio.

The [GGUF converter's model card](https://huggingface.co/audio-cpp/audio.cpp-gguf) points to the original checkpoint's license and says conversion does not change the governing license. Stability AI's [license FAQ](https://stability.ai/license) also lists Stable Audio 3.0 and discusses output ownership. Local candidate masters, processing recipes and exact files are kept out of the game importer and source control pending audition and release clearance.
