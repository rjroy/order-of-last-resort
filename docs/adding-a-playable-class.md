# Adding a playable class

Use this runbook for a hero who can be recruited, equipped, and played through a commission. Work from the repository root with the existing checkout. Read [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), the [game guide](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/README.md), and the relevant game records before changing the game. The examples use the shipped Hobby Smith; substitute the new hero's IDs and fixtures, not the Smith's mechanics.

## 1. Settle the play and delivery scope

Write down the class's job in a three-hero party: what decision earns it a place, what it does on its own turn, and why the player would sometimes spend the company's **one direct command** on it. Specify the starting HP, Move, offense, utility, available standing orders and default, legal targets and tie-breaks, use limits, and effects on later turns. Describe two concrete rounds: one where bringing the hero pays off and one where it does not. Start with an agreed encounter and three existing slots; roster growth does not require new missions or a fourth slot.

Decide whether the class is a founder or a paid recruit, what training or temporary rewards it can earn, and whether any other hero can borrow its actions. Agree on the budget and prerequisites against the existing economy: a road win pays 1 point, a destination win pays 5, and earlier road wins are retained on return even if the party loses the finale. A completed five-mission commission pays 9. Avoid changing those rules or existing guilds just to fit a new price. A standalone example gives quick access for testing; it is not evidence that the guild unlock works.

Use the retained [discovery](../.agents/skills/discover-game-hook/SKILL.md) and [mechanic exploration](../.agents/skills/explore-mechanic/SKILL.md) procedures for a new mechanic, not to reopen settled decisions. Record the chosen behavior, unresolved judgments, human contributions, and approved implementation scope. Concept, editable model, and runtime integration are separate approvals. A request to design, model, or review does not itself authorize the others, a new dependency, or a release.

## 2. Make the hero playable in data and rules

Author the smallest playable kit first; exercise it through a standalone battle and then the guild. Follow the [catalog guide](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/content/README.md) rather than duplicating loader rules.

| Where | What to change and check |
|---|---|
| [`content/heroes/`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/content/heroes/) | Add a hero file with its class where applicable and commandable `characters` entry on team `heroes`, with capabilities and the effective base `abilities`. Register the new file in the explicit `parts` array in [`content/catalog.json`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/content/catalog.json). Add ability `label`, `help`, `handler`, `params`, and `targeting`; put executable logic in a supported handler, not label text. `orders` defines labels, `habits` maps order IDs to behavior IDs, and `default_order` must select a compatible habit. Character overrides replace entire fields, including lists and maps. Character, class, order, ability and encounter placement IDs identify different things even if some happen to share a name. Keep shipped IDs stable. |
| [`content/expedition/commissions.json`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/content/expedition/commissions.json), [`guild_progression.json`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/content/guild/progression.json), [`guild_training.json`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/content/guild/training.json) | Add the character ID to `commission_settings.recruitment_roster` without colliding with encounter placements. Add `guild_progression.kits` with `offense` and `utility` (Move is separate). For a paid recruit, add a `petitions` entry with `cost` and a `grant` of `kind: "recruit"` naming the character's `hero_id`. Learned abilities grant a slot choice, not an automatic equipment change or the donor's orders, passives, or rewards. List petition IDs in `prerequisites`; put new training definitions in the training part. Check each available order against each supported kit. |
| [`content/expedition/rewards.json`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/content/expedition/rewards.json), [`adventure.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/expedition/adventure.gd) | If the hero has rewards, author `upgrades` with the character `hero_id`, `rarity`, `cap`, supported `effect`, `amount`, and readable copy. Use `requires_abilities` or `requires_any_ability` as appropriate for kit-dependent offers; don't give reward actions or orders in the base kit. Check offer eligibility, continuation gating, only claimed rewards counting toward caps, next-mission delivery, and removal on return. Preserve missing HP on max-HP gains. No outing offer after the destination, on defeat, or in standalone. |

The Smith example is character `blacksmith`, purchase `recruit_blacksmith`, offense `blacksmith_hammer_time`, utility `blacksmith_try_this`, and default `order_try_this`. `blacksmith_learn_acid` grants `alchemist_acid` to the offense slot and requires `recruit_blacksmith` and `recruit_alchemist`; **Edit loadout** equips it. `order_pursue` uses the equipped offense rather than hardcoding Hammer Time.

**Use the same rule path for the board, forecast, and commit.** In [`rules.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/rules/rules.gd), `state_before_action` accounts for earlier activations; use that context for legal choices and standing-order intent. Extend [`rules/actions.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/rules/actions.gd) and [`rules/behaviors.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/rules/behaviors.gd) only where existing targeting or AI handlers cannot express the design. Validate new definitions in [`content/catalog.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/content/catalog.gd). Spell out who can be selected (including self, civilians, minions, obstacles and corpses when applicable), what happens on ties, and whether an order waits, moves, or chooses another legal target. Do not silently retarget an invalid explicit command. No forecast refresh should spend an action, mutate the board or statuses, or reroll a chosen outcome. Commit the forecast through the real round path; use structured events for feedback, not a second simulation in the UI.

**Define lifetimes and interactions.** Say when a new state is applied, consumed, skipped, cleared, or carried; prefer the existing combat, movement, status and upgrade seams to new state copies. Test relevant intersections: reflection/redirect/retaliation, displacement and blocked landings, knockdown versus upright skip, damage versus protection/healing, defeat triggers, summons and mission objectives. A pressure plate or escort can change even in a round where the intended action succeeds. Test both the legal and the unavailable branch and check the actual post-round state, not only text.

**Protect existing saves.** Ownership, points and preferred loadouts live in [`guild_profile.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/guild/guild_profile.gd); [`guild_progression.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/guild/guild_progression.gd) resolves owned and equipped choices. Use the existing purchase and profile-save paths. For an ordinary paid recruit do not edit `FOUNDERS` or the legacy normalization roster; no save rewrite is needed merely to add a petition. Verify a fresh guild can only see/buy what it should, that an existing pre-class profile retains its name, progress, roster, purchases and preferences, and that the new purchase and equipped kit persist after relaunch. Check unmet prerequisites, insufficient funds, no double charge, and successful deployment by order. If the schema or defaults really change, add explicit compatibility and failure tests; do not replace or delete a player's save. Wounds and orders can carry within an outing, but mission statuses clear and outing rewards reset on guild return. There is no mid-run save. Standalone does not use the guild profile; custom catalog guilds use a separate profile. Use disposable fixture paths or isolated user data for all tests.

## 3. Build the coverage matrix while implementing

Keep a small matrix in the work record: **actual wearer and kit; source ability and resolved form; direct/order and initiative context; target/effect and fallback; forecast/commit result; actual drawn clip/recipient and sound; fixture/command and result**. Include one row per new usable action and per materially different equipment, branch or presentation path. Group identical paths and say why. The expected claim for each row should be observable, e.g. “earlier fitting makes Pull legal; the Knight moves the chosen target one tile, not himself.”

| Always decide or check | Add rows when the class uses this seam |
|---|---|
| Base kit and standing orders: legal/illegal action, no legal target, replacing an order with a direct command, another hero taking the command, repeatable forecast and identical commit. | New targeting, action-time buffs or randomness: earlier actor changes the board; tied, unreachable or blocked targets; explicit target becomes invalid; stale plan; repeat preview/clear/re-stage without consuming a roll or resource. |
| **Compatibility with the shipped Hobby Smith:** for every usable ability in the new hero's starting, trained or reward kit, record whether it uses an existing approved `fitted_form`, gets a newly approved one, or stays **ordinary-only**. Exercise the new hero beside a Smith, not just alone. | For a conversion, check earlier/later Smith, direct and standing paths as supported, changed legal targets, successful spend, and an unavailable alternate falling back to the ordinary action without spending the fitting. For an ordinary-only action, explicitly document and test the ordinary fallback: the legal ordinary action runs as usual, does not spend the fitting, and emits no converted cue. Normal mark cleanup still applies. The current picker does not automatically label an action without `fitted_form` as a converted fallback; make the limitation clear in the action help or play instructions. Move and Hold do not spend the mark. No blanket requirement to add a conversion for every ability or reproduce the Smith's original 25 forms. |
| Actual performer, aim, and resolved recipients are separate. Check that fallback uses the ordinary action's rules and feedback, even if the wearer still has a mark. | If training borrows a source ID or grants it to someone else, purchase, equip and deploy it on that **actual wearer**. Check the wearer's own upgrades and art/audio, not the donor's. For secondary effects such as riposte, echoes, splash, delayed reactions, saved doses or companion choreography, identify what is and is not another performance; check against duplicate effects or cues. For example, ordinary Repeat Batch can replay the special delivery of a stored fitted dose; this is not a new conversion. |
| Guild lifecycle and basic figure presentation: recruit/equip, use in battle, return/reload, relevant assets resolved. | If shipped, cover projectile release/impact, multiple recipients, different facings, recover vs defeat/held poses, moved or replaced entities and identity continuity, or an outcome transitioning off the battle page during playback. |

Write controlled, seeded rule fixtures with test-owned HP, positions, kit and expectations. Use integration tests for live authored data and public launch/catalog contracts, not to pin every tuning value. Reuse [`scene_fixture.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tests/support/scene_fixture.gd) and [`storybook_driver.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tests/support/storybook_driver.gd) for bounded interaction through real controls. Keep high-value regressions at the layer that failed; do not copy a whole fixture suite to test a shared behavior again. The [Smith verification record](../.lore/work/games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/notes/hobby-smith-verification.md) shows a completed matrix. Its Smith-specific capture drivers are evidence tools for their fixed fixtures; build a focused class-specific fixture with shared helpers if needed, not a generalized Smith tracing harness.

## 4. Produce and integrate the book, sprite and audio assets

**Model one character at a time.** Follow [model-a-class](../.agents/skills/model-a-class/SKILL.md) and the [art direction](../.lore/work/games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/art/direction.md): show the concept that establishes silhouette and kit, then build the self-contained rigged Blender file from the accepted version. Reopen its exact saved file; verify prop attachment, clearance, neutral rest, and contrasting actions. Review a rendered pose sheet at consistent tactical angle and scale and preserve the exact accepted source and provenance. Stop at model acceptance if runtime work is not authorized. A source render, motion preview or user approval of an illustration is not evidence of what Godot draws.

**Publish paired atlas and manifest entries.** Read the [cast recipe](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tools/cast/README.md) for current tool versions and constraints (Python 3, Blender 5.2.2 LTS and ImageMagick 7 for the verified bake). Use `tools/cast/actors/<role>.json` for a new role; pin approved source paths, SHA-256, rig and Action names. Check Ready, move, action/contact, hit/down/recover and defeat as the kit needs them. Choose sample frames/holds and anchor against tile scale, not atlas rectangle centers. If a prop releases, bake the departure offset from that exact pose, remove the held copy at the right frame, and let the board move the detached drawing to the true recipient. Use a separate supplemental recipe when adding approved motions to an existing wearer; compare original clip metadata and registered pixels against an unchanged stock baseline before publishing. Do not edit accepted masters to make a bake pass.

From the repository root, a staged build of the existing Smith role looks like this. Use a disk-backed scratch path and an empty stage; the staged manifest is partial.

```bash
(
  set -eu
  tools=games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tools/cast
  mkdir -p .lore/local
  scratch=$(mktemp -d "$(pwd -P)/.lore/local/class-bake.XXXXXX")
  mkdir -p "$scratch/tmp"
  export TMPDIR="$scratch/tmp" MAGICK_TEMPORARY_PATH="$scratch/tmp"
  python3 "$tools/bake_subset.py" "$scratch/stage" blacksmith --threads 3
  python3 "$tools/verify_cast.py" --assets "$scratch/stage" blacksmith
  printf 'Verified stage: %s\n' "$scratch/stage"
)
```

Publish only the approved PNG and matching actor entry in `assets/cast/manifest.json`, not the partial manifest; keep unrelated actors unchanged. The character record opts into cast art (`cast_key`, `cast_art_key`, and any needed team/variant constraints); sharing a class or an ability ID does not imply opt-in. Follow [`cast_cues.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/presentation/cast/cast_cues.gd) for supported ordinary and specialized bindings and [`cast_playback.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/presentation/cast/cast_playback.gd) for timing. Guard lookups by actual action, handler and wearer; preserve custom/no-art fallback. Use committed actor and recipient IDs and action-time cells for cues, not final round positions. Check at board scale, including adjacent actors and overlays; schedule animation without delaying or changing settlement.

**Update player-facing copy and art together.** Check the recruitment folio, deployment order, loadout compatibility and help text; make range, special targeting, statuses and order/command differences understandable on the battle board, in inspection, and in Forecast. Update the [help overlay](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/presentation/help/help_overlay.gd) and [help captures](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/assets/help/README.md) only when the instructions change. The [guild art resolver](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/presentation/book/guild_book_art.gd) reads packaged PNGs: `assets/guild-book/classes/` uses the character's explicit `guild_portrait`, `petitions/` uses the petition ID subject to existing explicit legacy aliases, and `upgrades/` uses the reward ID. Check `CLASS_LABELS` if the portrait needs a class subtitle distinct from the character name. Preserve approved native art and crop recipes. A petition preview neither equips nor buys anything; a reward offer should keep copy, cap and claim control visible. Check actual layouts at **1280×820 and 960×615**, with long copy, one and several offers, resize/focus, mouse and keyboard purchase/claim, and reachable Continue after settlement. Use the [art review recipe](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/assets-source/guild-book/README.md) for inventory sheets; they are not runtime layout evidence.

**Route sound from committed beats.** Use [`sound_effects.gd`](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/presentation/audio/sound_effects.gd) and existing recipes rather than triggering audio in the rules or the picker. Verify intended wearer/phase, distinguish action contact from release/landing/reaction where it matters, and prevent duplicate generic and specialized cues. Verify Master/Music/Sound effects behavior; previews of abilities and forecasts stay silent. The normal pencil feedback on staging an authored move is distinct from action playback and not a failure of forecast silence. Use approved sources with recorded provenance and applicable release terms; an audition of a candidate WAV does not clear it for shipping. The Smith shipped authored synthesis for its native cues rather than uncleared audition WAVs.

**Import and inspect exactly what will ship.** Run the headless editor import below after adding assets, scripts or shaders. Keep Godot-generated `.uid` sidecars with scripts/shaders, including tests; move each sidecar with its source. Retain tracked texture/audio import settings with accepted assets. Review `git status` and the intended diff for omitted metadata and unintentional churn; never include `.godot/`, scratch review media or user profiles.

## 5. Verify in the running checkout and hand off

Run from the repository root with Godot 4.7. These are working example commands, not a complete test list. Replace the party and loadout only with valid authored character and ability IDs. A mission with slots needs exactly one distinct commandable hero per slot in deployment order; an authored default party is used if `--party` is omitted. `--loadout` requires `--mission`, can select an authored slot or order without a guild purchase, and does not grant passive techniques or outing upgrades.

```bash
game=games/company-of-unlikely-heroes
godot --headless --editor --path "$game" --import
godot --path "$game" -- --mission=hold_the_line --party=blacksmith,knight,healer
godot --path "$game" -- --mission=hold_the_line --party=blacksmith,knight,healer \
  --loadout=blacksmith:offense=healer_bonk,order=order_pursue
```

In the first launch, leave default orders, choose the Knight's **Push → Pull**, and forecast the Bruiser at G3 landing at F3; clear to compare fitted Guard, restage Pull, then commit and inspect the result. The second launch exercises the Smith's purchased-kit equivalent without changing a guild. For the new hero, provide a similarly exact opening with a target, a clear expected result, and Restart. Also start a normal guild (no mission flags), buy/recruit through the game, play into a reward if applicable, and return/relaunch using test-owned saves.

For Linux headless and graphical review, run Godot with fresh user data rather than your normal desktop profile. The following runs representative checks; use a new sandbox for a fresh-guild review or any recorder that changes preferences. Resolve the binary before changing XDG paths (set `GODOT_BIN` to its absolute path if a shim requires normal preferences).

```bash
(
  set -eu
  game=games/company-of-unlikely-heroes
  godot_bin=${GODOT_BIN:-$(type -P godot)}
  mkdir -p .lore/local
  sandbox=$(mktemp -d "$(pwd -P)/.lore/local/class-review.XXXXXX")
  mkdir -p "$sandbox"/{data,config,cache,state,tmp,captures}
  export XDG_DATA_HOME="$sandbox/data" XDG_CONFIG_HOME="$sandbox/config"
  export XDG_CACHE_HOME="$sandbox/cache" XDG_STATE_HOME="$sandbox/state" TMPDIR="$sandbox/tmp"
  for suite in content/catalog_bundle_test journeys/commission_scene_test guild/guild_full_catalog_test \
    presentation/standalone_loadout_scene_test presentation/guild_compact_layout_test presentation/stock_action_sound_test; do
    "$godot_bin" --headless --path "$game" --script "res://tests/$suite.gd" \
      > "$sandbox/${suite##*/}.log" 2>&1
  done
)
```

Run focused rule, reward and presentation regressions from the matrix as well; use `guild_profile_test.gd` for touched save contracts and `help_book_scene_test.gd` for touched help (follow the game guide's separate fresh `OLR_TEST_SANDBOX` setup; X11 with XTEST is only for the OS-delivered F1 check). Read logs for parse, resource and renderer errors even when assertions pass. For a compact guild review, use a fresh copy of the sandbox setup above and replace the test loop with `"$godot_bin" --path "$game" --resolution 960x615`; confirm the actual viewport size. A recorder should use the [capture sandbox helper](../games/company-of-unlikely-heroes/tests/support/capture_sandbox.gd) and any stricter tool requirements; verify Godot's actual `user://` is in scratch before touching preferences. On other platforms use an isolated OS profile or an explicitly configured scratch profile with separately isolated preferences. Normal desktop launch without isolation is for the user's own play, not scripted verification.

**Prove actual play, not just scheduling.** In a supported visible display with a real audio backend, stage through the game's controls, verify silence outside intentional planning feedback, then Commit. Capture the real viewport after draw at relevant markers and settled frames. Inspect the intended actor's motion, target or landing cell, prop handoff, status/hit/heal feedback, and readability at play size. Record the game bus, not desktop audio; use a wearer-specific stem or an uncontaminated timing window when claiming that wearer's cue made sound. A mixed WAV peak doesn't identify the sound, and request logs/headless timing do not prove either pixels or audio. Record display, backend, viewport, command, build, fixture/seed and any limitations. Use a normal visible window; the Smith checkout's Wayland/PulseAudio recipes are in the verification record. A hidden X11 `--wid` parent crashed that setup. If live verification cannot run, mark it outstanding rather than substituting a headless pass.

**Close only what the evidence supports.** Share the direct local launch, what to try, the applicable fresh/existing-guild checks, and the durable matrix of pass/fail/blocked results. Ask the player whether they could identify who acted, who was affected, what changed and whether choosing this hero was worthwhile; record a human verdict separately from machine checks and from source approvals. Do not present one person's playtest as general enjoyment evidence. Preserve accepted sources, recipes/provenance, runtime assets and sidecars, tests, approvals and relevant work records; leave generated captures and logs under `.lore/local/`. Update the active work tracker if used and checkpoint only within the authorized scope; historical issue IDs are provenance. Don't make an archive, push or release in place of a working local checkout.
