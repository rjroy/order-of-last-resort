# Field report sketch map

This is the content and drawing plan for the child scout's field reports in *Barnaby’s Order of Last Resort*. The source is `../../../content/expedition/commissions.json`, `mission_reports`. The two variants for a mission share the same image. No image uses a future mission, the other branch, an onward kind, or destination-only copy. The 13 illustrated transit reports appear in commission forks; for the nine finales this set covers their authored immediate report records, not a new fork.

The cheap cream school paper is part of each opaque PNG. A child of about six drew a single oversized observation from the encounter in dark, shaky pencil and uneven wax crayon. Shapes overlap and perspective is inconsistent; no polished lighting, caption, labels, numbers, or writing. Leave blank paper on all sides. Use wide crops contained within 510 × 212 at 1280 × 820, preserving the reviewed bridge plate's roughly 3:2 aspect when practical. Keep only features common to the two public variants, backed by the immediate encounter.

## Transit

### `bridge_patrol` · sheet A top left

1. Two Harpooners wait on opposite sides of the road. Each can hit from two tiles away, so check both before stepping between them.
2. These harpoons wound but don't pull. Stay more than two tiles from both raiders, or close on one without stepping into the other's reach.

Two tiny raiders poke huge straight harpoons toward the same empty road. Empty space is the observation. No hook rope or dragged figure; no bridge promised by the internal ID.

### `goblin_mob` · sheet A top right

1. Five goblins are closing from both sides. They hit harder when two get beside the same target.
2. The goblins are weak on their own. Watch the gaps a second goblin could use to join the first.

Two goblin heads with absurdly long green ears crowd a little shield from either side. This is a close-up of a pair, not a total head count.

### `harpoon_squad` · correction sheet top left

1. A Hookhand and a Harpooner cover the lane. The Bruiser is already close enough to make a bad pull hurt.
2. The Hookhand can drag a hero within reach of the slower Bruiser. Look at where the hero would land, not just where they stand.

A bent hook and long line tug a little hero by the cape toward a Bruiser with a disproportionately big fist. No other threat, range diagram, or landing tile.

### `light_hook_pair` · accepted standalone sketch

1. A Hookhand and a Harpooner cover the lane. The Hookhand can pull a hero closer while the Harpooner strikes from a distance.
2. Watch where the Hookhand's pull will land your hero, and check the Harpooner's reach before closing.

Two travelers stand apart, each holding an upright pole. The blunt-ended crook looks comically bent next to the straight pointed pole. No Bruiser or action; the report text explains the pull.

### `shield_wall` · sheet A bottom left

1. Two Shieldbearers flank an Archer. Each one beside the Archer cuts the damage the Archer takes.
2. The Archer is the threat at range. Separate each Shieldbearer from the Archer to break that protection.

Two lopsided upright shields almost hide a skinny Archer. The crooked wooden bow still peeks out between them.

### `cowardly_captain` · sheet B top right

1. The Captain tries to back away when heroes get beside him, but the two Grunts hit harder at his side.
2. The Grunts aren't stronger everywhere. It's standing next to the Captain that puts extra force behind their blows.

A small, plume-helmeted Captain has a broad Grunt close at either shoulder with silly big spears. Focus on the Grunts' closeness and force, not the Captain fleeing.

### `explosive_situation` · sheet A bottom right

1. Two Bombers go off when they fall. A blast hits anyone next to the Bomber, even its own allies.
2. Watch the Harpooner's two-tile reach when picking a spot clear of the blast. The Bombers can set each other off while they're side by side.

Two side-by-side Bombers caught in the same oversized red-orange zigzag. No Harpooner, wound, blast radius, or depiction of what set it off.

### `crossfire_blast_lane` · accepted standalone sketch

1. A Hookhand can pull a hero toward the Bomber, which can strike up close before it falls. Its death blast hurts everyone beside it, friend or foe.
2. The Harpooner strikes from two tiles away while the Bomber closes in. Check the Hookhand's pull and the Bomber's blast before committing.

One round plum-and-umber fellow stands on a crooked ochre path with a curled, unlit cord on his cap. The round coat and little cord are the observation. No other figure, movement, or blast.

### `unfortunate_healer` · sheet B bottom left

1. Two Bruisers have a Healer who can patch up a wounded ally within two tiles.
2. The enemy Healer has only two heals. A Bruiser out of reach gets neither.

One tiny Healer reaches to wrap a nearby Bruiser's enormous mitt. Close-up, not a head count, and no literal charge or distance markers.

### `duelists` · sheet B bottom right

1. Two Duelists are waiting. A Duelist who survives a hit can strike back if the attacker is still beside them.
2. These two punish a clean hit at arm's length. Attack from farther away or leave a gap before the return blow.

Two Duelists crowd the picture in different poses; show one spear flicking back at a nearby attacker. Emphasize the bent shaft or little return-action scribble, not injury.

### `odd_couple` · sheet C top left

1. The Trickster can trade places with the Ogre once. Watch for it to move closer to your company than the Ogre is.
2. The Ogre hits hard up close. A gap that looks safe from the Ogre may not be safe from a swap.

A thimble-sized Trickster and enormous Ogre connected by a pair of crooked sweep marks showing exchange. No exact tile or use count.

### `mirror_company` · sheet C top right

1. They brought a Knight who pushes, a Wizard who blasts the nearest character, and a Healer who patches up wounded allies.
2. The rival Healer gets two heals. Their Wizard can hit their own side, and their Knight answers a taunt.

The three rivals together, united by the child's mismatched grouping: conspicuous shield, enormous pointed hat and crooked staff, and the Healer's spool of bandage. No target, trigger, or charges.

### `last_one_standing` · sheet C bottom left

1. When a Bandit falls, the Fierce one hits harder and the Swift one moves farther. Those gains stack.
2. The Guard Bandit shrugs off part of a heavy hit once another Bandit is down. Small hits still get through, and the armor only improves once.

A bandit with the biggest bedraggled hat has toppled over. Two other bandits with matching hoods perk up at the fall. No speed, armor or damage markers; their response to an ally falling is the shared observation.

## Finales

2. Get the ferryman to the far bank by the end of round ten. Clearing the Harpooner alone won't get him to the ferry.

A ferryman with a comically long oar toes a bank, with a little Harpooner across a gap. No crate, exact timing, or path marked.

### `unwelcome_guest` · correction sheet top right

1. The troll follows the nearest living hero and stops beside them. Draw it out of the marked square.
2. A shove will do if the troll ends the round outside the square. It has to survive the round.

A living troll puts one ridiculous foot across a crooked square outline. No suggestion that the troll fell, left by a unique tactic, or was killed.

### `wrong_side_of_the_door` · sheet D bottom left

1. The plate at C2 opens the gate at D3 while any living character stands on it. Send a hero through to F3.
2. A goblin waits beyond the gate. The plate works under a goblin too, and the gate closes as soon as the plate is empty.

A giant boot weighs on a flat stone disc beside a visibly raised barred gate. No exact coordinates, no goblin solution or exit route.

### `hold_the_line` · sheet C bottom right

1. Goblins close in from both sides with a Bruiser to the east. Keep one hero alive through round six, when help arrives.
2. From round four, an Archer and a War Drummer join the attack when their entry tiles are clear. You don't need to defeat them to hold the line.

One lingering hero peeks out behind a shield drawn absurdly wide. A few incoming action scratches show pressure without identifying an enemy, aid, or timing.

### `great_sheep_escape` · sheet E top left

1. All three sheep need to reach the east pen alive. At round's end, loose sheep react to the nearest non-sheep within two tiles.
2. A sheep that can't get farther from whoever startled it may step toward the pen instead. Once penned, it settles.

A single sheep in close-up becomes a giant wool cloud just inside a little pen. No asserted total, specific push path, or turn counter.

### `bridge_is_out` · connected study report plate

1. The C span drops at the end of round four and the E span at the end of round six. Don't be standing on either when it goes.
2. Get a living hero to G3 by the end of round eight. You can leave the raider behind; reaching the bank is what matters.

A failing crossing and distant bank, reduced to wobbly lines and one outsized loose plank. No opponent, turn count, tile labels, exact collapse order, or casualty.

### `dont_wake_the_giant` · sheet E top right

1. The treasure is at E3. Each hero move adds one noise, another ability adds two, and a performance adds three.
2. Holding is silent. Even if a hero reaches the treasure early, the round has to end at twelve noise or less.

Treasure chest and one tiny figure taking an exaggerated careful tiptoe, with a finger to their lips. No noise tally, guard, or awakened giant.

### `unfortunate_ritual` · sheet E bottom left

1. All three circles must have living occupants at the end of two rounds in a row. Enemies and summons count.
2. A Hookhand can spoil your placement. One empty circle at round's end resets the count.

A pair of immobile boots planted inside one emphatic wonky chalk circle. Close-up with no asserted total, occupation recipe, Hookhand, or round count.

### `last_wagon` · sheet E bottom right

1. Two raiders are coming for the wagon. A living enemy next to it stops it from moving that round.
2. Keep the route clear and the wagon can take one step at round's end. It has to reach the village intact.

Big cargo wagon with disproportionate wheels, with a muddy, empty ribbon of road in front of it. Focus on clearing the wagon's way, not the number or position of raiders.
